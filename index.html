Evylanta Academy

Practical learning for M&E and MEL teams.

Learning content below is a starter curriculum. Each lesson includes a short study note and a completion action. A verifiable certificate requires a real user account and server-side course records; the prototype can generate a sample completion certificate.

Module 1: M&E fundamentals

Understand monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning.

Module 2: Theory of Change & logframes

Map the pathway from activities to outcomes and impact.

Module 3: SMART indicators

Define clear indicators, baselines, targets and disaggregation.

Module 4: Data collection methods

Choose surveys, interviews, observation and routine records.

Module 5: Data quality assurance

Check validity, completeness, consistency, integrity and timeliness.

Module 6: Analysis & visualization

Summarize results responsibly and select appropriate charts.

Module 7: Reporting & learning

Write evidence-based findings, recommendations and learning notes.

Module 8: Ethics & safeguarding

Consent, confidentiality, data minimization and safe handling.

Connect Evylanta to Supabase

First connection step. Enter the Project URL and Publishable key from your Supabase project. These are browser-safe values when your database is protected by Row Level Security (RLS). Never use or enter a service_role/secret key here.

This version only tests and saves the connection settings on this device. Account sign-up, cloud data syncing and shared workspaces are not switched on yet; those are the next implementation step.

Evylanta account

Real account sign-in. Use the Project URL and Publishable key already saved in Connect cloud. Create an account with your email and a new password. Never share your password with anyone.
Tap Show to view your password, or Hide to conceal it.

This step connects account authentication only. Your projects, indicators, forms and submissions are not yet syncing to the database. After sign-up, check your email if Supabase email confirmation is enabled.
