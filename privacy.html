Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Evylanta is a monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning (MEAL) tool for NGOs, community organisations, consultants and field teams. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you use Evylanta, why, and what choices you have. The service is operated by Evylanta ("we", "us"), based in Kenya.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account we receive your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email on your Google account. We also keep the role and workspace choice you make inside the app.
  • Organisation membership. If you create or join an organisation workspace, we store the organisation name, your membership, your role and any access requests.
  • Your project data. By default, projects, indicators, data records, forms, submissions, tasks, feedback logs and reports are stored in your own browser on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers. If you choose to share a project to a cloud workspace, that project's information is also stored in our cloud database so it can sync across your devices and with the people you add. Projects you do not share stay on your device only. Clearing your browser data or changing device can remove locally stored projects, so please use the backup and export tools regularly.
  • Workspace and team information. If you create or join a workspace, we store the workspace name, your role, and the names and email addresses of members. Workspace administrators and project managers can see the names, emails and roles of people on their projects. Invite codes, their expiry and who used them are also stored.
  • Technical information. Your browser stores a sign-in session so you stay signed in. Our hosting provider may log technical details such as IP address and browser type when you load the site.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide organisation workspaces, roles and access approvals.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the service safe and working.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Who processes your information

We do not sell personal information. We use these service providers to run Evylanta: Supabase (sign-in, workspace data and the project data you choose to share; project region: Northeast Asia, Tokyo, Japan), Google (only if you choose Sign in with Google), and GitHub Pages (website hosting). Some of these providers process data outside Kenya. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Information about the people you work with

When your organisation uses a cloud workspace, your organisation decides what information is collected, who is invited and what each person can see. We store and process that information on the organisation's behalf to provide the service. Access is limited by role: data collectors see only the forms assigned to them and their own submissions, while managers and officers see the data of the projects they belong to.

If you use Evylanta to record information about beneficiaries, patients or other people, you are responsible for having a lawful basis, getting any consent that is needed, and collecting only what you need. Avoid recording names, contact details or sensitive details unless essential, and follow your organisation's data protection and safeguarding rules. Do not record serious safeguarding concerns in the app; follow your organisation's reporting procedure.

5. Retention and security

We keep account and organisation information while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards. You can ask us to delete your account. Data in a cloud workspace is kept until the workspace administrator removes it or asks us to delete it. We use encrypted connections and access rules on our database, but no system is completely secure, so please protect your password and the device you use.

6. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict its use, and ask for a copy in a portable form. To use these rights, contact us at evylantasupport@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

7. Children

Evylanta is intended for adults working in professional settings and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as Evylanta grows, for example when project data begins to sync to the cloud. We will change the date above and, where the change is important, tell you in the app.
